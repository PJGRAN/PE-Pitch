The Reality Today

Every portfolio company is running on
5 to 12 disconnected systems of record.

Acquisition cost
Every acquisition loses 3 to 6 months to data archaeology — manually extracting, cleaning, and reconciling data across ERP, CRM, and spreadsheets before a single insight is possible.
Hold period cost
Portfolio reporting relies on quarterly Excel exports that are already outdated when they arrive. Value creation decisions are based on data that is weeks old.
Exit cost
Buyers discount companies with fragmented data. Months of pre-exit data cleanup reduce the time available to grow the business and compress the achievable multiple.

The root cause is not a lack of tools. It is the architecture: multiple systems of record that were never designed to share a common data model. Adding another integration layer does not solve this. It compounds it.

The Paradigm Shift

We don't integrate existing systems.
We replace them.

The standard consulting answer is to build an integration layer on top of the existing stack. We take the opposite position: integration layers preserve the complexity that created the problem in the first place.
