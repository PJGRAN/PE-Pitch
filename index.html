The Situation

Every portfolio company is running on
5 to 12 disconnected systems of record.

Acquisition cost
Every acquisition loses 3 to 6 months to data archaeology. Before a single insight is possible, someone has to manually extract, clean, and reconcile data across ERP, CRM, and spreadsheets.
Hold period cost
Portfolio reporting runs on quarterly Excel exports that are months old by the time they arrive. Value creation decisions are made on data that does not reflect the current state of the business.
Exit cost
Buyers discount companies with fragmented data. Pre-exit data cleanup takes months and compresses the achievable multiple. The problem should have been solved at acquisition, not in the final year of ownership.

The root cause is not a lack of tools. It is the architecture: multiple systems of record that were never built to share a common data model. Adding another integration layer does not solve this. It makes the problem harder to undo.

Our Approach

We don't add another layer on top.
We replace the stack.

The standard answer is middleware. Connect SAP, Salesforce, and Excel via an integration layer, and report from the top. That preserves every inconsistency in the underlying systems. We take the opposite position.

Why This Works

We map the business before
we touch the technology.

Replacing systems of record is only safe if you understand the underlying business logic first. Our method enforces that sequence and does not allow a technology decision until the problem and process are fully defined.

Step 01
Problem
Every operational problem is decomposed into a structured issue tree. No technology decision is made until the problem is precisely defined and agreed.
Step 02
Workflow
The process is mapped in full: every step, decision point, handoff, and data flow. This becomes the specification the platform is built against.
Step 03
Resource
Human, software, or AI is assigned to each step based on what fits best, not on what is available or habitual. Legacy systems are replaced only once the workflow is fully mapped.
